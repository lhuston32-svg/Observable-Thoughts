About Observable Thoughts

Observable Thoughts creates modern geometric wall art designed to transform with light, movement and viewing angle.

Each piece combines bold colour, layered patterns and optical effects to create artwork that appears to shift, react and reveal new details as its surroundings change.

About the Art

The designs use geometry, contrast, repetition and visual layering to create depth and movement on a flat surface.

Changes in lighting and viewing position can reveal different colours, shapes and patterns, allowing the same artwork to produce a different visual experience throughout the day.
